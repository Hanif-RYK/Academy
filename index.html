FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is there a free trial class?

Yes. Every new student can join one free trial class before taking admission.

What are the class timings?

Classes run Monday to Saturday between 2:00 PM and 8:00 PM. Weekend batches are also available.

How will I know about my child's progress?

You receive a monthly report with test results and teacher feedback, plus a parent-teacher meeting every term.

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Yes, the second child gets 10% off the monthly fee.
